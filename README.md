## 👋 About

I help B2B companies stop losing leads to slow, manual work. My main focus is **real estate agencies**: a lead that waits is a lead that buys from someone else, so I build the systems that answer in seconds, qualify, and book the visit — then plug them into the CRM and the ads that feed them.

Everything below is built and run on my own self-hosted stack (VPS + Docker + n8n), the same one I deliver to clients.

---

## 🛠️ What I build

| | Service | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 💬 | **WhatsApp AI SDR** | Answers every lead in seconds, qualifies with a sales method (SPIN), follows up and books meetings on Google Calendar. |
| 📣 | **Paid traffic** | Meta and Google Ads campaigns built around the full funnel — audience, targeting and conversion — not just "boost post". |
| 📍 | **Google Business Profile** | Setup and ongoing management of the local profile that brings in "near me" searches. |
| 🌐 | **Landing pages** | Simple, fast pages that send the visitor straight to the conversation, with no form in the way. |
| 🧠 | **Complete commercial system** | CRM + WhatsApp + n8n + AI agent + reporting panel, installed and operated end to end. |
| 🔎 | **Free diagnosis** | A short look at how fast and how well your leads are answered today, before anything is sold. |

> Every project is priced case by case, by the result it delivers.
